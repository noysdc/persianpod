# Casa Milan Persian Podcast کازا میلان

The Casa Milan Persian Podcast [CMPP] , initiated in 2020 by Parsa Karami, is the first Farsi-language podcast focused on AC Milan. Aimed at the Iranian community of dedicated AC Milan fans, our goal is to cover all things related to the club in a concise and informative manner. Throughout this journey, my primary objective has remained to support and unite the Iranian Milan community.

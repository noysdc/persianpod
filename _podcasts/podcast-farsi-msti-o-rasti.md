# پادکست فارسی مستی و راستی

Welcome to Masty o Rasty "The Drunken Truth" podcast. In this show I tell some crazy, funny and tragic stories of my life as a nomadic artist and cultural miscreant. I sometimes even have guests who are brave enough to get as intoxicated as I am and delve into the taboo issues of our culture. Come on this ride as we shed light on the dark night of the soul.

# همراه و همگام با دکتر محمدی

We all strive to live a better life. This can be having a better relationship with our kids, spouse, parents, family, coworkers, friend or with ourselves. Dr. Mohamadi dives into different psychological and social issues and conditions in each episode, to help you have a better understanding about your mental make up and help you live a better life.
